Your voice stays yours.

What ShoutAbout does with your voice and your words.

Last updated: 11 October 2026

The short version

  • We don't collect your data. No server of ours receives your voice, your words or anything about you.
  • Your audio and text stay on your devices and your own network between them.
  • No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. This website sets no cookies.
  • The apps go online only to download the speech model (once), and for options you set up.

Who we are

ShoutAbout is made by Spencer Seay, an independent developer (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the ShoutAbout apps for Windows, Mac and iPhone, and this website, shoutabout.fyi.

Your voice and your words

On your computer, ShoutAbout listens only while you dictate: between two presses of your key, or while you hold it. A speech model on your computer turns the recording into text, which is pasted where you're typing. Recordings aren't kept after they're turned into text. One that couldn't be turned into text is held in memory so you can try again, until ShoutAbout quits.

On your iPhone, the app records only when you start it (with the mouth, the Action Button, or one of its controls or shortcuts) and stops when you tell it to. It turns the recording into text with Apple's on-device speech recognition; it never asks Apple's servers to do it. Or, if you chose your computer, it sends the recording there.

Between them, your iPhone connects straight to your computer over your own network and sends the recording (or, on slower computers, the finished text), and your computer sends back the text. Nothing passes through us or any cloud service. On your local network this connection isn't encrypted, so use it on networks you trust. Your computer accepts it only from your local network, and only from iPhones you connected yourself.

We can't see any of this, and you can delete it any time.

Word suggestions. To help it spell names right, ShoutAbout on your computer can suggest words from the names of your project folders. It only looks at them when you ask it to. This happens on your computer, and nothing is added without your OK.

Removing it. On Windows, uninstalling asks whether to delete your settings and words too. On a Mac, drag the app to the Trash and delete ~/Library/Application Support/ShoutAbout to remove them. On iPhone, deleting the app deletes its data.

When the apps go online

  • The speech model. On first start (or when you pick a different quality), your computer downloads it from Hugging Face, which sees your IP address and the request. No audio or text is sent. On iPhone, iOS may download its on-device speech model from Apple, under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Finding your computer. Your computer announces its name on your local network so your iPhone can find it; other devices on that network can see it. It includes no keys or personal data beyond that name.
  • Nothing else, by default. The apps send us no analytics, crash reports or usage data, and don't check for updates in the background.
  • Options you set up yourself. If you connect your computer to the internet through your own Cloudflare Tunnel, dictations sent from away go through Cloudflare under your Cloudflare account and its terms. The command-line developer tool that builds iPhone Shortcuts can send the shortcut's layout (its steps, your computer's addresses and your home Wi-Fi name, never keys, audio or text) to a signing service; it runs only when you run it, and it can be switched off.

The clipboard. On your computer, ShoutAbout pastes through the clipboard and then puts back what was there. On your iPhone, results are copied to the clipboard; with Apple's Universal Clipboard, iOS may share it with your other Apple devices.

This website

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other companies. It's hosted by Cloudflare, which processes your IP address and request details to deliver the pages and protect them from attacks; we don't use that to track you. The Windows and Mac downloads are served by GitHub, under GitHub's privacy statement.

App Store and TestFlight

Apple handles the iPhone app's download and your Apple account. If you've chosen in iOS settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with us; they don't contain your recordings or your text. If you join the TestFlight beta, Apple shares the name and email address you were invited with, and any feedback you send through TestFlight.

If you email us

We see your email address and your message, use them only to answer you, and delete them when they're no longer needed.

Children

ShoutAbout isn't aimed at children under 13. We don't collect personal data, so we don't collect any from children either.

Your choices and rights

Everything ShoutAbout keeps is on your devices, where you can view, change or delete it. We hold no data about you, except messages you've sent us (by email or as TestFlight feedback), which we'll delete if you ask. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California), you have rights over personal data that companies hold about you; you can always contact us about them.

Changes

If ShoutAbout starts handling your data differently, we'll change this page first and update the date at the top.
