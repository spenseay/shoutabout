Three ways to use it.

On your computer, on your iPhone, or both together. Switching takes one tap.

  • Right Ctrl Right ⌘

    Talk to your computer

    Press Right Ctrl (Right ⌘ on a Mac), talk, press it again. Your words appear wherever you're typing: email, docs, chat.

  • 0:07 ComputerPhone Listening

    iPhone on its own

    Press the Action Button or tap the mouth, and talk. Your iPhone writes it down by itself and copies it. No computer needed.

  • ComputerPhone Pasted!

    iPhone → computer

    Talk into your iPhone, and it's typed on your computer over your home Wi-Fi. Away from home, it lands on your iPhone's clipboard instead.

Ready in about a minute, with no account. See the setup guide.

Questions?

Is it really free?

Yes. ShoutAbout is free to download and use. No ads, no account, no trial, no in-app purchases.

What does it run on?
  • Windows: Windows 10 (version 1809 or later) or Windows 11, 64-bit. An NVIDIA graphics card makes it faster, but any PC works.
  • Mac: a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
  • iPhone: iOS 26 or later. It starts as a TestFlight beta; the App Store comes later.
Does my voice go to the cloud?

No. Speech is turned into text on your computer or on your iPhone. When your iPhone sends a recording to your computer, it goes straight across your home Wi-Fi. We never receive your audio or your words.

Do I need the iPhone app?

No. Your computer works on its own with a key, and the iPhone app works on its own too. Use both, and your iPhone becomes a microphone for your computer.

Why does Windows warn me when I install it?

Windows doesn't know ShoutAbout's installer yet, so it says “Windows protected your PC”. Click More info, then Run anyway. A Microsoft Store version, which installs without the warning, is planned. The Mac app is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens without one.
