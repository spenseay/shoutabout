Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no account, no trial, no in-app purchases.

Is it private?

Yes. Speech is turned into text on your computer or your iPhone, and we never receive your audio or your words. See the privacy policy.

What does it run on?
  • Windows: Windows 10 (version 1809 or later) or Windows 11, 64-bit. Any PC works; an NVIDIA graphics card makes it faster.
  • Mac: Apple silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
  • iPhone: iOS 26 or later (iPhone 11 and newer).

It understands English.

Does it need the internet?

Only once, to download the speech model. Your iPhone sends to your computer over your home Wi-Fi; away from home, it writes the text itself and copies it.

How do I uninstall it?
  • Windows: Settings › Apps › Installed apps › ShoutAbout › Uninstall.
  • Mac: quit ShoutAbout, then drag it from Applications to the Trash.
  • iPhone: delete the app.
Is it open source?

Yes. The code is on GitHub under the MIT License. The open-source parts it uses are on the licenses page.

Quick fixes

My iPhone doesn't find my computer

  • Both on the same Wi-Fi, with ShoutAbout running on the computer.
  • Windows: your Wi-Fi must be a Private network. Settings › Network & internet › Wi-Fi › your network › Network profile type › Private network.
  • iPhone: Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network › ShoutAbout on. On a Mac with macOS 15 or later, the same switch is in System Settings.
  • Still nothing? Scan the code instead: in the app, More options.

Nothing appears when I talk

  • Is ShoutAbout paused? Choose Resume from its icon.
  • Just installed? It says “Getting ready” while the speech model downloads.
  • Mac: System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility › ShoutAbout on.
  • Windows: apps running as administrator don't accept pasting. Press Ctrl+V: the text is on your clipboard.

“Windows protected your PC”

Click More info, then Run anyway.

It keeps spelling a name wrong

Choose Teach words from ShoutAbout's icon and add the name the way it should be written.
