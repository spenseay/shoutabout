Set up in a minute.

Pick how you'll talk. You can add the others later.

Talk to your computer

Press a key, talk, press it again.

  1. Windows

    Install

    Download ShoutAbout for Windows and open ShoutAbout-Setup.exe. Windows asks first: click More info, then Run anyway. Then Yes, Next and Finish.

    Mac

    Install

    Download ShoutAbout for Mac, open ShoutAbout.dmg and drag ShoutAbout into Applications. Open it, and click Open when macOS asks.

    Allow the microphone, and turn on ShoutAbout under Accessibility so it can paste.

  2. Open it

    Getting ready

    ShoutAbout downloads the right speech model for your computer.

  3. Try it

    Say something

    Press Right Ctrl (Right ⌘ on a Mac), say the line, press it again.

  4. Done

    Talk anywhere

    Press your key in any app, talk, press it again.

iPhone on its own

Your iPhone writes the words down and copies them. Works anywhere.

  1. Open the app

    Where should your words go?

    Get the iPhone app, open it, and tap This iPhone. Then allow the microphone.

  2. Optional

    One press to talk

    Open Settings › Action Button, swipe to Controls, tap Choose a Control, then pick ShoutAbout › Dictate.

    Then press the Action Button, talk, and press it again. No need to open the app.

    No Action Button? Add Dictate to Control Center instead.

  3. Talk

    Tap & talk

    In the app, tap the mouth and talk. Tap again to finish. Your words are on your clipboard.

iPhone → computer

Talk into your iPhone, and your computer types it. Both need to be on the same Wi-Fi.

  1. On your computer

    Connect your iPhone

    At the end of setup, or later in Settings › Add a phone.

  2. On your iPhone

    Tap your computer

    Open ShoutAbout and tap My computer (or Settings › Connect a computer). Tap your computer when it shows up.

    When iOS asks about your local network, tap Allow.

  3. Both

    Check the code, click Allow

    Your iPhone and your computer show a 4-digit code. If they match, click Allow on your computer.

  4. Talk

    Choose your computer and talk

    At the top of the app, choose your computer instead of Phone. Tap the mouth, talk, tap again. Your words appear wherever you're typing on your computer.

  5. Away from home

    It still works

    If your computer can't be reached, your iPhone writes the text itself and puts it on your clipboard.
