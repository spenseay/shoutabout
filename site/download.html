Release notes and SHA-256 checksums

Downloading

Open ShoutAbout-Setup.exe when it's done. Windows asks first (see below). Then click Yes, Next and Finish: ShoutAbout starts next to the clock and walks you through the rest. See the steps.

Windows will ask first

Until the Microsoft Store version is ready, Windows shows “Windows protected your PC” when you open the installer, because it doesn't know ShoutAbout yet. Click More info, check the app is ShoutAbout-Setup.exe, then click Run anyway. If your browser says the file isn't commonly downloaded, choose Keep.

On PCs with Smart App Control turned on, Windows blocks the installer without a Run anyway button. The Microsoft Store version will fix that too.

  • Windows 10 (version 1809 or later) or Windows 1164-bit.
  • About 1.1 GB of disk spaceplus the speech model, downloaded once on first start (up to 1.6 GB, depending on your PC).
  • NVIDIA graphics card: optionalIt makes ShoutAbout faster. Any PC works.
  • A microphoneto talk to your PC, or the iPhone app.
  • Remove it any timeSettings › Apps › Installed apps › ShoutAbout › Uninstall.

Coming soon

ShoutAbout.dmg · Apple silicon · signed and notarized

Release notes and SHA-256 checksums

Downloading

Open ShoutAbout.dmg when it's done and drag ShoutAbout into Applications. Open it, then click Open when macOS asks about an app from the internet. A yellow mouth appears in the menu bar and walks you through the rest. See the steps.

Checked by Apple

The Mac app is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so there are no security warnings to get around: the first time, macOS just asks whether to open an app from the internet. Then it asks once for the microphone and once for Accessibility, which is what lets ShoutAbout paste where you're typing.

  • A Mac with Apple siliconM1 or newer.
  • macOS 14 Sonoma or later
  • About 500 MB for the speech modeldownloaded once on first start.
  • Remove it any timeQuit it from the menu bar, then drag ShoutAbout from Applications to the Trash.
